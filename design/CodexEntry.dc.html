Canon Overview

Born of a possession ritual gone catastrophically right, the Withered Host is what remains when a Deadite fully consumes its vessel — no longer a haunted body, but a standing wound in reality that wears a person's shape out of habit rather than need. It hunts in packs of memory, drawn to places where grief has worn the world thin.

Designer's Notes
Statted as CR 7 to sit a notch above a standard Fiend of its size — the source material's escalating, unkillable-feeling threat is represented through Magic Resistance and the teleport-based Legendary Action rather than raw damage, so it reads as relentless without out-damaging the party's healing.
DM Eyes Only
The Host is not one entity but a chorus — every vessel it has ever worn still speaks through it, and a PC who listens too long (Wisdom save) may recognize a voice they lost. First seeded through a sealed archive beneath Morrow's Edge; its true weakness is never mentioned in the stat block above.
